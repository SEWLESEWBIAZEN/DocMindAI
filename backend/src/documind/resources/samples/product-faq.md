# Product FAQ — Aurora Smart Thermostat

*Sample document provided by DocuMind. The product is fictional.*

## Installation

**Which heating systems does Aurora support?**
Aurora works with most 24 V systems: gas and oil boilers, heat pumps (single stage and two stage)
and forced-air furnaces. It does not support 230 V line-voltage electric heaters.

**Do I need a C-wire?**
A C-wire is recommended. Without one, use the included power adapter kit.

**How long does installation take?**
About **30 minutes** for most homes. The app guides you step by step.

## Using Aurora

**How do I set a schedule?**
In the Aurora app, open *Schedule*, choose the days and drag the temperature points. Schedules sync
to the thermostat within one minute.

**What is Eco mode?**
Eco mode lowers the target temperature by **3 °C** when nobody is home, based on phone location
and the motion sensor.

**How do I reset Aurora to factory settings?**
Press and hold the dial for **10 seconds** until the screen shows *Reset*, then choose
*Factory reset* and confirm. This removes your schedules and Wi-Fi settings.

## Warranty and support

**What does the warranty cover?**
Aurora has a **2-year** limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Damage from incorrect
wiring is not covered.

**How do I make a warranty claim?**
Contact support with your order number and a photo of the wiring. Claims must be made within the
warranty period.

**When is support available?**
Phone and chat support are open **Monday to Friday, 08:00–20:00**, and **Saturday, 09:00–14:00**
(Central European Time).
