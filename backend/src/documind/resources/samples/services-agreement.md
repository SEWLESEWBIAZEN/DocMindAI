# Master Services Agreement — Brightline Analytics and Harbor & Pine Retail

*Sample document provided by DocuMind. The companies and terms are fictional.*

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between **Brightline Analytics Ltd.**
("Provider") and **Harbor & Pine Retail Inc.** ("Customer").

## 1. Term and renewal

1.1 The Agreement starts on **1 March 2026** (the "Effective Date") and runs for an initial term of
**24 months**.

1.2 After the initial term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive **12-month** periods,
unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **90 days** before the end of the
current term.

## 2. Services

2.1 Provider will deliver demand-forecasting dashboards for Customer's 140 stores, refreshed daily
by 06:00 local time.

2.2 Provider will deliver a **quarterly performance report** within **15 business days** after the
end of each calendar quarter.

2.3 Customer will provide point-of-sale data exports every night by 02:00 local time. Delays in
these exports extend Provider's delivery times by the same amount.

## 3. Service levels

3.1 The dashboards will be available **99.5%** of each calendar month, excluding scheduled
maintenance announced at least **5 days** in advance.

3.2 If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, Customer receives a service credit of **5%** of
that month's fees for each full 0.5% below the target, up to **25%** of the monthly fees.

## 4. Fees and payment

4.1 Customer pays a monthly fee of **USD 18,500**, invoiced on the first business day of each month.

4.2 Invoices are payable within **30 days** of the invoice date. Late amounts bear interest at
**1% per month**.

4.3 Provider may increase fees once a year, by no more than **4%**, with **60 days'** written notice.

## 5. Data protection and security

5.1 Provider processes Customer data only to provide the services and keeps it in data centres in
the European Union.

5.2 Provider will notify Customer of any personal-data breach **without undue delay and in any event
within 72 hours** of becoming aware of it, by email to Customer's security contact.

5.3 Provider will complete an independent security audit every **12 months** and share the summary
report with Customer within **30 days** of receiving it.

## 6. Liability

6.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid in the **12 months**
before the event giving rise to the claim.

6.2 The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality, data-protection obligations or
indemnities.

## 7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not remedied within **30 days**
of written notice.

7.2 On termination, Provider will return or delete Customer data within **45 days**, at Customer's
choice, and confirm deletion in writing.

## 8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **England and Wales**.
