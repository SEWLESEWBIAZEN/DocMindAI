# Employee Handbook — Leave, Remote Work and Expenses

*Sample document provided by DocuMind. The company and policies are fictional.*

This handbook applies to all employees of **Harbor & Pine Retail Inc.** from **1 January 2026**.

## Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public
  holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount.
- New employees accrue leave from their first day; in their first year they may take up to
  **10 days** before their six-month review.
- Request leave in the HR portal at least **2 weeks** in advance for absences longer than three days.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next year; they must be used by **31 March**.

## Sick leave

- Tell your manager before your usual start time on the first day of absence.
- A doctor's note is required for absences longer than **5 consecutive working days**.

## Parental leave

- Employees with at least **6 months** of service receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave.
- Give HR at least **8 weeks'** notice of the planned start date where possible.

## Remote work

- Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, agreed with their manager.
- Customer-facing store roles are excluded from remote work.
- Remote workers must use the company VPN and a company-managed laptop.
- A one-off home-office allowance of **USD 400** is available after the probation period.

## Expenses

- Submit expense claims within **30 days** of the expense, with receipts, in the HR portal.
- Claims above **USD 500** need approval from a department head before the expense.
- Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.
